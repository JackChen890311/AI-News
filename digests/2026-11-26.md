### 2. [Lazy Agents Answer Against Their Own Beliefs: Reinforcement Learning with Verifiable Beliefs Teaches Them to Act on What They Believe](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.32884)

> 💡 該研究解決了 AI 代理人盲目猜測的「懶惰」習慣，提供了一種高效且具擴展性的通用代理人後訓練（Post-training）解決方案。
